# Enhance Your Workspace with a Keyboard Tray: A Comprehensive Guide for Remote Workers and Work-from-Home Professionals

Are you tired of having your keyboard and mouse crammed together on an unstable desk surface, or worse – sitting too close to your eyes? Do these issues cause discomfort and distraction? If yes, then I have the solution you’ve been searching for – a Keyboard Tray. In this article, we will explore why keyboard trays are essential for any home office setup, how they can improve comfort and efficiency, as well as provide some recommendations on which models to consider.

## Why Use a Keyboard Tray?

A keyboard tray is designed to elevate your computer peripherals away from your body. It provides a comfortable ergonomic posture by reducing the pressure points around the wrists, shoulders, and neck. By adjusting your desk height for a more ergonomically correct position, you can significantly reduce strain on these areas which are often neglected in traditional home office setups.

### Ergonomic Benefits of Keyboard Trays

- **Reduces Neck Strain**: Keeping your mouse and keyboard elevated reduces the need to bend forward as much, thereby decreasing stress on your neck muscles.
- **Decreases Shoulder Pain**: Elevating your arms can help prevent shoulder tension caused by extended periods of typing or using a mouse.
- **Improves Wrist Comfort**: Proper elevation helps alleviate the pressure on the tendons that control hand movement and reduces strain associated with repetitive motions.

## How to Choose the Right Keyboard Tray

Selecting an appropriate keyboard tray requires considering several factors including size, material, adjustability, and functionality. Here's a breakdown of these considerations:

### Size
- **Height**: The most critical factor is how high the tray needs to be from your desk surface.
- **Width**: Ensure it fits comfortably in front of your monitor or screen.

### Material
- Consider whether you prefer an aluminum or plastic model as each has its own advantages. Aluminum trays are typically sturdier and more durable, while plastic models might be lighter and easier to clean.

### Adjustability
- Adjustable height is important if you use the keyboard tray in conjunction with a monitor stand for better screen positioning.
- Rotational capability allows you to place your mouse closer or farther from the keyboard without moving the tray itself. 

### Functionality
- Some trays come equipped with additional features like cup holders, USB charging ports, or adjustable height levels.
