### Understanding Your Lighting Needs

Before we dive into the best desk lighting options, it's essential to understand what type of lighting works best for you. There are three main types of lighting: task lighting, ambient lighting, and accent lighting.

*   **Task lighting** focuses on a specific area, reducing eye strain and improving visibility.
*   **Ambient lighting** provides overall illumination, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.
*   **Accent lighting** highlights specific features or areas, adding visual interest to your space.

To boost productivity, you'll want a combination of these lighting types. Consider the activities you'll be performing, the color temperature of the light, and the level of brightness that works best for you.
