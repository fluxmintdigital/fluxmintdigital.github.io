# Best Mechanical Keyboards for Home Office
If you're looking to upgrade your home office setup and want something as professional-grade as it is comfortable, consider investing in a mechanical keyboard. These keyboards offer precision and tactile feedback that can make typing more enjoyable and efficient, especially when working from home. In this guide, we’ll explore some of the best options available for home office use.
## Key Features to Look For
When shopping for a mechanical keyboard, there are several features you should consider:
- **Keycaps**: Some keyboards offer various keycap styles like cherry red or tactile, which can impact the feel and look of the keys.
- **LED Backlighting**: Ideal for dark environments where visibility is important. This feature can come in handy when working late nights or during power outages.
- **Switch Types**: Common mechanical switch types include Cherry MX, Opto, and IBM 5200. Each type has its own characteristics, such as key travel distance and actuation force, which affects typing speed, accuracy, and fatigue levels.
## Best Mechanical Keyboards for Home Office
### Logitech G915 Lightspeed Pro
The **Logitech G915 Lightspeed Pro** is a top choice for home office users. Its RGB backlighting can be customized to match your workspace or chosen accent color scheme. The keyboard's switch types include both optical and membrane options, allowing you to choose the tactile feedback that suits your preference best.
### Corsair K70
The **Corsair K70** is another great option with its minimalist design. It features a custom Cherry MX Red mechanical switch for precision and accuracy in typing. The keyboard also comes equipped with Corsair's Vortex RGB backlighting system, which gives you complete control over the colors and effects.
### Cooler Master MasterMouse 3500
The **Cooler Master MasterMouse 3500** is designed to handle even the most strenuous home office workloads. It offers a range of switch types from Cherry MX Red to Tactile, providing various levels of tactile feedback and key travel distances.
